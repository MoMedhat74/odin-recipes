Fish

Description

This swai fish dish is delicious. I typically do not like fish; however, I love this one! It is light-tasting and, in my opinion, does not have a fishy taste. I serve this fish with English peas or green beans and wild rice or rice pilaf

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 4 (4 ounce) fillets swai fish
  • 2 tablespoons margarine
  • ¼ cup dry white wine
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro (Optional)
  • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika

Steps

    step 1

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a shallow pan or baking sheet with cooking spray.

    2. Step 2

  2. Place fish fillets into the prepared pan

    3. step 3

  3. Melt margarine in a saucepan over medium heat. Mix white wine, lemon juice, cilantro, garlic, salt, and black pepper into melted margarine; simmer for 2 minutes.

    4. Step 4

  4. Generously spoon sauce over fish fillets. Sprinkle fillets with paprika.

    5. Step 5

  5. Bake in the preheated oven until fish flakes easily with a fork, 10 to 12 minutes.
